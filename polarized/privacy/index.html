POLARIZED

Privacy policy · Last updated 17 August 2026

Polarized is a browser party game. There is no account to create, no password, and no email address. This page describes everything the game stores.

What we collect

  • The display name you type when you join a room. Call yourself whatever you like — it is shown to everyone else in the room.
  • An anonymous player ID. When you open the game your browser is issued an anonymous identity by Supabase, our database provider. It is what lets the server tell players apart and stop anyone editing somebody else's vote. It carries no name, no email address and no password, you never see it, and it is not linked to you, your device, or any other room.
  • Your votes — agree or disagree — and the statements the moderator types.
  • The room code and the scores for that game.

We do not ask for your email address, we do not collect payment details, and we run no analytics or tracking software of our own.

What stays on your device

The game keeps your player ID, your display name, the code of the room you are in, and the vote you cast in the current round, in your browser's local storage. That is what puts you back in your seat if you reload the page or your phone dies mid-game. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Who can see your votes

Votes are hidden until the moderator reveals the round. After the reveal, everyone in the room sees who voted which way — that is the game. Treat a vote as visible to the room, not as anonymous.

Anyone holding a room's four-letter code — or the invite link that carries it — can join that room. Codes are short by design, so do not put anything in a statement you would mind a stranger reading.

Retention and deletion

Rooms are temporary. A room and everything attached to it — players, votes, statements, and scores — is deleted automatically within 24 hours of the room being created.

The anonymous ID described above outlives the room, because it is what puts you back in your seat if you reload. It is deleted automatically once it has gone seven days without being used. Losing it costs you nothing: the next time you open the game your browser is simply issued a new one. There is nothing to log into and nothing else kept.

To have a room removed sooner, email privacy@planitnow.us with the room code.

Service providers

Each of these processes data only to provide its part of the game:

  • Supabase — database hosting and the anonymous IDs above
  • Cloudflare — website hosting
  • Google AdSense — advertising

The page also loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Supabase client library from jsDelivr. Like any request to any website, those receive your IP address and browser details.

Advertising and cookies

Polarized sets no cookies of its own. It uses local storage, described above, and nothing else.

Ads come from Google AdSense and appear only on the lobby screen and the final-score screen — never during a round, and never on a voting or reveal screen. Where ads are shown, third-party vendors including Google use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Children

Polarized is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. Since we collect no contact details, there is no account to close; if you believe a child's information is in a room, contact us and we will delete the room.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact

Polarized is operated as an independent project and is reachable at privacy@planitnow.us.
